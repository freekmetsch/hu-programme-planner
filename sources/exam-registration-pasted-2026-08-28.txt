Deadlines 2026-2027
Below, you will find the deadlines for the academic year 2026-2027.

Starting from the academic year 2026-2027, the registration period for exams with a scheduled exam date has changed. This change provides you with more time to register for exams that have a specific date. If you wish to participate in an exam, you must register in Osiris. You have up to three weeks before the exam to do so.

Beginning on Wednesday, July 1, 2026, you can register for courses and tests in blocks A to D of the academic year 2026-2027. The registration period for block E starts on Monday, September 1, 2026. You can immediately register for your first and second exam opportunities. If you pass the first exam opportunity, your registration for the second exam opportunity will be automatically deleted. So, make sure to enroll for the entire academic year right away!

For exams in block YEAR, the exam registration closes on August 31, 2027.

Please be aware that different deadlines apply during the Christmas period and in block E! These deadlines are shown in the blocks below for the different periods.


There are 2 different types of exams:

Exams without a specific exam date (such as a report or presentation)
Exams with a scheduled exam date in your timetable (such as a written or digital exam)
Please note: do you have an exam, for example a report, and can't find it in MijnRooster? The deadline is Wednesday in block week 5 (with the exception of block E: the deadline is Saturday, August 31, 2027).

Please note: For exams managed by the programme and not listed in Osiris Rooster, check MijnRooster to see if the exam date is visible. If you are unsure about the submission deadline, please ask the teacher.

Please note: For some courses for Human Resource Management, different registration deadlines apply. Registration opens on March 1, 2027. Registration closes according to the regular deadlines, which are shown in the blocks below for the different periods.
